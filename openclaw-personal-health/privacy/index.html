Privacy Policy

This policy describes how OpenClaw Personal Health (the "App") handles data. The App is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Giacomo Dotto for his own use only.

Data accessed

With the user's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the App requests read-only access to the following Google Health data:

  • Activity and fitness data (scope googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly)
  • Sleep data (scope googlehealth.sleep.readonly)
  • Health metrics and measurements, such as heart rate, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, temperature and weight (scope googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly)

How data is used

Data is used only to display and summarize the owner's own health information in his private, self-hosted assistant. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit, insurance or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Data and OAuth tokens are stored only on devices controlled by the owner. Tokens are kept in private configuration files and are never published.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it and can be deleted at any time by removing the local data files. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the App's Google consent screen.

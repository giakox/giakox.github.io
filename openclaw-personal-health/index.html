OpenClaw Personal Health

OpenClaw Personal Health is a personal, non-commercial tool used by its owner, Giacomo Dotto, to synchronize his own health and fitness data from Google Health into his private, self-hosted assistant.

It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What it does

  • Uses Google OAuth 2.0 to request read-only access to the owner's Google Health data: activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements.
  • Reads the data through the Google Health API and stores it only on the owner's own devices for personal review.
  • Never writes, modifies or deletes data in Google Health.
